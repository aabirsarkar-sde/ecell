How to read an entry

Every idea carries three judgements: who signs the cheque, how much steel it takes before revenue, and how long until a first real customer. The hard part line is the honest one — it names the thing most likely to kill the company, not the thing that sounds impressive in a pitch.

What this isn't

Not validated businesses, not market research, and not a claim that nobody is working on these. Several have Indian companies in them already; where that's true, the entry points at the part still unbuilt. Treat the catalogue as a set of prompts to argue with.

The bias in the list

Chosen for one filter: things that are easier, cheaper or only possible to build in India, because of a policy window, a demand pattern, a cost structure or a talent pool that exists here and not elsewhere. Ideas that could be started anywhere were left out.
